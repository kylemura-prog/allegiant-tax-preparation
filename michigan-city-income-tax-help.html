Michigan city tax questions

The city name is only the starting point.

Have a different question? Text (616) 541‑5158 or email anytime.

Which Michigan cities impose an income tax?+

Michigan Treasury currently lists 24 cities that levy municipal taxes related to income. Filing rules, forms, administration, rates, and deadlines can vary by city and tax year, so the applicable city and year must be confirmed before preparation.

Do I need a city return if I live outside the city but work there?+

Possibly. A nonresident filing requirement may apply when income is earned from work or services performed inside a Michigan taxing city. The city, work location, workdays, withholding, and tax-year rules must be reviewed.

What if I moved into or out of a taxing city during the year?+

A part-year city return may be required. Exact move dates, addresses, income timing, work locations, and city withholding help determine the proper reporting for each part of the year.

Can remote work affect a Michigan city return?+

Yes. For nonresidents, the location where services were actually performed may affect city-tax allocation. Employer records, work calendars, and the applicable city's instructions may be needed.

Can Allegiant prepare prior-year Michigan city returns?+

Yes, when the city, tax year, records, forms, and filing method are within the agreed scope. Older or unsupported returns may need to be printed, signed, and mailed.

Does city tax preparation include responding to a notice?+

Not automatically. Preparing a return and reviewing or responding to a city notice are separate services unless both are specifically included in the written scope. See the tax notice review process.
