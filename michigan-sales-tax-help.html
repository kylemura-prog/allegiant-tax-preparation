Common questions

What to expect.

Can you help if filings are behind?

Yes. We first identify the affected periods and available records, then quote catch-up work separately from future filing support.

Does paying the tax replace filing a return?

A payment does not replace a required return. We compare filings and payments separately when reviewing the account.

Will a notice balance disappear?

The result depends on the records, required filings, and Treasury review. You receive an explanation of the findings and next steps.

Do you handle every state or complex sales tax issue?

This service focuses on Michigan. Multistate nexus, audits, appeals, and complex exemption issues require a separate scope and may need a referral.
