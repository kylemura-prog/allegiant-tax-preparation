LLC startup questions

Know what the service does—and does not—include.

Have a different question? Text (616) 541‑5158 or email Allegiant.

Is the Michigan filing fee included in the $249?+

No. Michigan's standard Articles of Organization fee is currently $50 and is separate from Allegiant's professional fee. State and expedited-processing fees may change.

Does the IRS charge for an EIN?+

No. The IRS issues EINs without a government application fee. Allegiant's package includes guided assistance so the responsible party can complete the application with the business information entered consistently.

Will forming an LLC automatically lower my taxes?+

No. An LLC is created under state law, while its federal tax treatment depends on the number of owners and any elections made. Allegiant explains the basic default treatment; any separate tax election requires its own review and authorization.

Is an operating agreement included?+

No customized operating agreement or other legal document is included. Allegiant does not provide legal advice. When legal drafting or advice is needed, the client should work with a Michigan attorney.

Can Allegiant help with a multi-member LLC?+

Potentially, but it is not covered automatically by the standard package. Multiple owners create additional legal, tax-return, recordkeeping, and agreement considerations, so the facts must be reviewed before scope and pricing are confirmed.

Does submitting the intake authorize a filing?+

No. The form is only a request for review. Allegiant will not file an LLC, apply for an EIN, make a tax election, or begin paid work until the scope, price, required information, and written authorization are completed.
