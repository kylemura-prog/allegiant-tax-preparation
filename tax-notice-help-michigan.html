Tax notice questions

What should I send for a notice review?+

Send every page of the notice, the envelope when the mailing date matters, the complete related tax return, and any documents tied to the issue. Do not send only the first page or a cropped screenshot if additional pages contain deadlines or instructions.

Can Allegiant review a state tax notice outside Michigan?+

Yes. Tax notice review is available remotely for IRS and state tax notices nationwide, subject to the specific agency, issue, records available, deadline, and service scope. Michigan city tax notices remain a particular local focus.

Should I pay the amount on the notice immediately?+

Payment depends on whether the notice is correct, the deadline, available records, and the taxpayer's circumstances. A review helps identify the issue and options, but it does not extend a deadline. Avoid ignoring the letter while information is being gathered.

Does a notice review include representation?+

No. The initial review explains the notice and next-step options. Response preparation, amended returns, evidence packets, or representation are included only when specifically available and confirmed in a separate written scope.

Can Allegiant guarantee the notice will be removed?+

No. Outcomes are controlled by the agency and depend on tax law, filed returns, available evidence, deadlines, and the facts of the case. Allegiant does not guarantee an adjustment, refund, penalty removal, or other result.

Can you review a City of Muskegon notice?+

Yes. A city tax notice review may include residency dates, work location, local wages and withholding, filed city returns, and supporting residency or employment records, depending on the issue.

What if the response deadline is close?+

Submit the complete notice immediately and clearly identify the deadline. Allegiant will confirm service availability, but the taxpayer remains responsible for protecting the deadline unless a separate agreement states otherwise.
