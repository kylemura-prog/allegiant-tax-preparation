Tax preparation questions

Know what to expect before you start.

Have a different question? Text (616) 541‑5158 or email anytime.

How much does tax preparation cost?+

Tax preparation starts at $195. Final pricing depends on the forms, state and city filing requirements, business or rental activity, condition of the records, review needs, and overall complexity. Pricing is confirmed before work moves forward.

Can Allegiant prepare my return remotely?+

Yes. Allegiant is based in North Muskegon and serves local clients, but the intake, secure document upload, and signature process also support remote clients nationwide.

Do you prepare Michigan city returns?+

Yes, when an applicable Michigan city return is part of the agreed filing scope. City residency, work location, and local withholding details may require additional documentation.

Can you help with self-employment or a side business?+

Yes. Schedule C and 1099 income can be coordinated with the personal return. Complete records are important, and bookkeeping cleanup may be quoted separately when the available records are incomplete.

Can Allegiant prepare unfiled prior-year returns?+

Yes. Allegiant prepares unfiled prior-year federal, state, and applicable city returns. Use the Prior-Year Return Request form and list the years you believe are missing. Kyle will review the available records, confirm the scope and price, and explain the filing process. Notice review or amendment work is separate when needed.

What should I provide from last year?+

A complete prior-year federal, state, and applicable city return is helpful for comparison, carryovers, dependent and credit history, and consistency checks. Provide it when available.

How long will my refund take?+

Refund timing is controlled by the IRS, state, or city agency and can vary because of processing, verification, offsets, or other review. Allegiant can confirm filing acceptance but cannot promise a refund date.
