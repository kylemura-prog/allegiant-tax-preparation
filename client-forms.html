MI
Michigan city income tax questions are built into the tax intake process

New and current-year personal clients can report city residency, work location, remote-work, and local withholding details in the Personal Tax Intake. Returning clients can report city-related changes in the Annual Update, and Schedule C clients can flag business activity performed in a Michigan city. If you are unsure which form applies, use Start Here.
