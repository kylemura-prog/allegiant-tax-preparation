Common questions

Know what to expect before you reach out.

Have a different question? Text (616) 541‑5158 or email anytime.

Do you prepare personal tax returns for individuals and families?+

Yes. Allegiant prepares federal and state individual income tax returns for individuals and families, along with applicable Michigan city returns. Remote service is available nationwide, and local clients are served throughout West Michigan.

How do remote and in-person appointments work?+

Allegiant is designed to work remotely through online forms, document uploads, text, email, and scheduled phone calls. Text and email are welcome anytime. Phone appointments are generally available after 3:30 PM on weekdays. In-person meetings are limited and must be arranged in advance.

Can you help if my bookkeeping is already behind?+

Use the bookkeeping inquiry to explain how far behind the records are and what information is available. Allegiant will review the situation before confirming the scope and pricing.

How is pricing determined?+

Individual tax preparation starts at $195. Monthly bookkeeping starts at $199, payroll support starts at $149, and combined bookkeeping and payroll starts at $329. Final tax-preparation pricing depends on the forms and filing jurisdictions required, self-employment, rental or investment activity, available credits, and overall complexity. Amended returns, prior-year returns, business returns, tax notices, and other specialized work are quoted separately. Allegiant confirms the price and required information before work begins.

What if I am unsure which form to use?+

Use the general service request form. Explain what you need, and Allegiant will point you toward the correct next step.
