Can I work with Allegiant remotely?+

Yes. Allegiant serves the Muskegon area locally and offers remote-friendly forms, secure uploads, phone, text and email communication.

Use the bookkeeping inquiry to explain how far behind the records are and what information is available. Allegiant will review the situation before confirming the scope and pricing.

Pricing depends on the complexity and scope of the tax, review, notice, bookkeeping, payroll or planning request. Allegiant confirms pricing and required information before work begins.

Use the general service request form. Explain what you need, and Allegiant will point you toward the correct next step.
