This policy explains how Allegiant Tax Preparation handles information collected through this website, online forms and related communications.

Please do not send sensitive information through ordinary channels.

Do not send Social Security numbers, bank information, passwords or sensitive tax documents by text, ordinary email or a general inquiry form. Use Allegiant's document-upload process only when Kyle requests it.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Allegiant Tax Preparation website, service-request forms, tax organizers, bookkeeping and payroll inquiry forms, document-upload process and direct business communications.

An engagement letter, consent form or other written client agreement may contain additional terms for a particular service. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices.

2. Information Allegiant may collect

Contact and inquiry information

This may include your name, business name, email address, telephone number, preferred contact method, service interests and the details you choose to provide about your situation.

Client and tax-service information

If you move forward with a service, Allegiant may collect information needed to evaluate or perform that service. Depending on the engagement, this may include tax, income, expense, bookkeeping, payroll, dependent, entity, filing-status, notice and prior-return information.

Documents and communications

Allegiant may receive documents you upload after being directed to the document-upload form, along with emails, text messages, telephone-call notes and other communications related to your request or engagement.

Technical information

The website host, font provider, Google Analytics and linked services may receive ordinary technical information such as browser and device details, pages requested, referring source, approximate location, timestamps and website interactions under their own policies.

3. Website analytics

Allegiant uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use this website and which service options receive interest. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies or similar identifiers and collect information about pages viewed, scrolling, outbound-link activity, browser and device characteristics, referring sources, approximate geographic area and the date and time of a visit.

Allegiant also measures clicks that open tax, bookkeeping, payroll and general inquiry forms, along with clicks to text or email Allegiant. These events identify the type of service or contact method selected; they are not configured to send names, email addresses, telephone numbers, form answers, tax information, uploaded documents or other sensitive personal information to Google Analytics.

Advertising-personalization signals and Google Signals are disabled in Allegiant's website tag. Google states that Analytics uses IP addresses during processing but does not log or store them. For more information, review Google's explanation of Analytics data safeguards.

You may limit Analytics by blocking or deleting cookies in your browser or by using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking Analytics does not prevent you from viewing the website or opening Allegiant's forms.

4. How information is used

Allegiant may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and identify an appropriate next step.
  • Evaluate, price, establish and perform requested services.
  • Prepare or support tax returns, bookkeeping, payroll, notice reviews and tax-planning work.
  • Communicate about documents, deadlines, appointments, billing and service status.
  • Maintain business, security, compliance and recordkeeping processes.
  • Protect Allegiant, its clients and others from fraud, misuse or security threats.
  • Comply with applicable law, professional obligations and valid legal requests.

Tax return information

Tax return information is subject to special confidentiality and use restrictions. Allegiant intends to use or disclose that information only for permitted tax-preparation and related purposes, with client consent when required, or as otherwise authorized or required by law.

5. Sharing and service providers

Allegiant does not sell client personal information. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with:

  • Technology and service providers used for website hosting, forms, document storage, communications, tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll or business operations.
  • Government agencies, taxing authorities, courts or other parties when authorized by the client or permitted or required by law.
  • Professional advisors or contractors who have a legitimate need for the information and are expected to protect it.
  • A successor in connection with a permitted sale, transfer or reorganization of the business, subject to applicable confidentiality requirements.

The website is currently hosted through GitHub Pages. Website measurement uses Google Analytics, and online forms and document uploads use Google services. Those services are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Facebook and other linked sites apply their own terms and privacy policies when you visit them.

6. Security and retention

Allegiant uses administrative, technical and physical measures intended to protect personal information in light of the sensitivity of the information and the size and nature of the practice. No website, email system, text message, cloud service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and tax records, meet legal or professional obligations, resolve disputes and protect legitimate interests. Retention periods can vary by record type and engagement.

7. Your choices and responsibilities

You may ask Allegiant to correct your contact information or discuss deletion of inquiry information that is no longer needed. Some records may need to be retained for legal, tax, security, insurance or professional reasons.

Please provide accurate information, use the form intended for your situation and follow instructions about where to send documents. Website inquiry forms are intended for adults. Information about dependents should be provided only when needed for an accepted client engagement and through the process Kyle identifies.

8. Changes to this policy

Allegiant may update this policy as services, technology or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
