Prior-year filing questions

Know what to expect before you start.

Have a different question? Text (616) 541‑5158 or email anytime.

Can Allegiant prepare more than one missing tax year?+

Yes. List every year you believe is unfiled. Each federal, state, and applicable city return is reviewed based on the records and filing requirements for that year, and the total scope and price are confirmed before preparation moves forward.

What if I am missing W-2s or 1099s?+

Start with the records you have. Employer or payer copies and IRS wage and income transcripts may help identify reported forms, but transcripts may not include every record needed for an accurate return, especially deductible business expenses or state and city information.

Can an older return be filed electronically?+

Electronic filing availability depends on the tax year, return type, software, and agency. Some prior-year returns can be e-filed while older or unsupported returns may need to be printed, signed, and mailed. The filing method is confirmed for each return.

Do I have to pay the entire balance before filing?+

Filing and paying are separate obligations. Allegiant explains the return result and filing steps, but clients remain responsible for making payments or establishing any agency payment arrangement unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

Does prior-year preparation include tax representation?+

No. This service covers preparation of the agreed missing returns. Tax-notice review, amended returns, evidence packets, collection matters, and representation are separate services and are included only when specifically available and confirmed in writing.
