Payroll questions

Know what to expect before payroll begins.

Have a different question? Text (616) 541‑5158 or email anytime.

How much does payroll support cost?+

Ongoing payroll support starts at $149 per month. Final pricing depends on employee count, pay frequency, work locations and tax jurisdictions, current records, setup needs, and the responsibilities included in the service agreement.

Can Allegiant help me switch payroll providers?+

Yes. A transition begins with reviewing prior payroll reports, year-to-date wages and taxes, employee records, account access, and outstanding filing or deposit responsibilities. Transition work is scoped before the change is made.

Who approves each payroll?+

The employer remains responsible for submitting accurate payroll information, reviewing the payroll preview, reporting corrections, and approving payroll by the agreed deadline. Allegiant does not guess at hours, pay changes, or employee information.

Are payroll tax filings and year-end forms included?+

The approved agreement identifies which calculations, filings, deposits, reports, and year-end forms are included. Responsibilities can vary based on the payroll platform, jurisdictions, account status, and service scope.

Can bookkeeping be included too?+

Yes. Business Essentials combines ongoing payroll and bookkeeping starting at $329 per month, with a $349 onboarding fee and three-month initial commitment.

Can Allegiant support payroll outside Michigan?+

Potentially, yes. Remote payroll support may be available nationwide, but availability depends on the employer's state registrations, employee work locations, tax jurisdictions, payroll platform, existing accounts, and overall setup. Allegiant reviews those details before confirming service.

Do we have to meet in person?+

No. Payroll setup and ongoing communication are designed to work remotely through secure records, forms, email, text, and scheduled calls. In-person meetings are limited and arranged in advance when needed.
